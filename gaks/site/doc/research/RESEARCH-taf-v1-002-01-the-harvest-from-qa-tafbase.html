What this is#

A previous-generation framework — a Java/Selenium/Cucumber suite (tests_base engine + per-product modules, JUnit4, Appium) — was mapped end-to-end for reusable design. This document keeps the result and the decisions it produced. The raw harvest — 27 ideas with evidence paths, translations and costs — is RESEARCH-taf-v1-003-01-the-harvest-in-detail.

The rules the harvest confirmed#

  • Declared beats interpreted. What the old runtime computed — device capability strings, tag→env mapping, layered properties — tafbase emits at codegen. The generator absorbs what a factory once computed.
  • Dwell is for observation, not synchronization. visibilitySleep (a global + per-call-site Thread.sleep around every action) answered two needs at once: letting JS settle and letting a human watch. Auto-waiting markers own the first; a declared dwell owns the second. They must not fuse — sleeps-for-correctness is the disease this framework is built to avoid.

The .srt item deserves a note: all five teardown paths of the old framework captured a video-stop timestamp and called a createSubTitlesFile that was only ever committed commented out — the intent was a subtitle track mapping each narrated action line (Clicking 'X' on 'Y') onto video timecodes. The hard half — timestamped narration — already existed. In generated code both halves are cheap: emitted steps know their text and their clock, so a teardown writer emits results/<runId>/<test>.srt beside the video. A run becomes replayable and readable.

Deliberately left behind#

PPTX reports, Robot/AWT screen capture, hotkey-driven video, Swing overlay strips, VM-awake mouse jiggles, X:-drive archiving, hardcoded notification tokens. Playwright viewport capture + HTML reports + committed artifacts replace the whole cluster.
