Definition#

The loop that grows the DSL: the language cannot say it → a test is authored by hand in custom/ → the manual pattern recurs or stabilizes → a model clause learns to say it (loader + both emitters + spec + a named refusal) → regenerate, and generated code replaces the manual code. custom/ trends back toward empty.

Why it exists#

A fixed vocabulary eventually fails — the SUT does something the step language never imagined. Two bad answers exist: freeze (the DSL rots) or grow custom/ forever (the DSL becomes a suggestion and the manifest stops describing the suite). The loop is the third: hand-code is scaffolding, the model absorbs it, and the language is the residue of everything that ever needed saying twice. The old framework ran the same metabolism informally — waitUntilVisibilityOfEitherElements and the ifPresent* idioms were born as hand-rolled helpers that recurred until they earned a name. Here it is deliberate: wait-any, maybe, soft expect, dwell are the loop already running, fed by the harvest rather than by custom code.

In this project#

custom/ is the seam (planned — the emitter never writes there, the manifest can mark source: hand). A growing custom/ is the DSL reporting which clause is missing — a diagnostic, not a failure.

Touch it#

The newest clauses are the proof:

grep -E 'wait-any|maybe|soft|dwell' suts/ikc3-tracker.sut.model.json
