Definition#

A {"dwell": <ms>} step — a declared pause, emitted as a literal page.wait_for_timeout(800) / page.waitForTimeout(800) at exactly the step where a watching human needs the screen to hold still.

Why it exists#

Automation that nobody can watch cannot teach. The old framework's visibilitySleep answered two needs with one sleep: letting the page settle and letting the eye follow. tafbase separates them — correctness belongs to markers and CONCEPT-playwright-v1-003-01-auto-waiting; observation belongs to dwell. Keeping them apart is the whole point: a sleep that papers over a timing bug is a lie the suite tells later.

In this project#

dwell is a validated step form (taf/model.py, bad-dwell refusal); both emitters render it; the tracker model pauses 800 ms inside open-tot so a watcher sees the tot island land. Suite-wide pacing is the environment's job — TAF_SLOWMO in CONCEPT-taf-v1-003-01-the-environment.
