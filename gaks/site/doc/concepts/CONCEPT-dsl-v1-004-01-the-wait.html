Definition#

Step forms that say how long to look. Implemented today: {"wait-for": {"element": x}} or {"state": y} — an auto-waiting assertion that must hold. Planned (lane H-8): {"check": ...}, the soft twin that collects a failure and continues, and {"wait-any": [...]} for UIs with alternating states.

Why it exists#

Most flakiness is timing that was never declared. A junior reaches for sleep; a named wait says the intent — hold hard until the marker appears versus log and continue. The old framework kept the whole vocabulary: waitUntilVisibilityOfElement, waitInvisibilityOfElement, waitUntilVisibilityOfEitherElements, with soft variants that logged, saved DOM and screenshot, and went on. Hard fails the step; soft records it. A suite needs both — "the SUT must be here" and "the SUT might offer this".

In this project#

wait-for is a step form in taf/model.py and both emitters (state → expect_arrived, element → to_be_visible). check and wait-any are lane H-8, drafted in PLAN-taf-v1-001-01.

Touch it#

Add {"wait-for": {"state": "tot"}} to a do list and run python -m taf check — the loader validates it like any step.
