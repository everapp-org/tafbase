Definition#

An expected artifact — a baseline screenshot — that a compare screen step diffs the actual against, plus the discipline around it: a filename grammar (TCID_run_res_step_{Expected,Actual,Diff}.PNG), tolerance and excluded regions per device, and human approval: a person promotes Actual to Expected via a selection file, never by auto-rebaselining.

Why it exists#

A picture asserts what fifty locators cannot. The old framework made screenshots the golden standard, with _ALT baselines for legitimate variants, exclusion masks for the iPhone notch, and a scanner that rendered a report grouping diffs. The approval loop is the non-obvious gem: goldens go stale because the SUT legitimately changed — a person must bless the new truth, or the suite silently re-baselines its own drift.

In this project#

Planned — lane J-10. The CONCEPT-dsl-v1-009-01-the-test-id and CONCEPT-taf-v1-004-01-the-artifacts layout land first, because the filename grammar needs both.
