Definition#

Verifying email: snapshot the inbox before the action, then poll only messages that arrived after a marked timestamp, comparing the body against a template whose volatile fields are masked (X_PLACEHOLDER_X, regex-masked Date:/Deposit ID:).

Why it exists#

Mail is the canonical asynchronous oracle — you can't wait for it, you can only look for what wasn't there before and verify it loosely enough that volatile fields don't flake it. The old framework's tricks were the reusable pearls: the baseline snapshot (only new messages count), the sparse poll schedule ({2,15,25,43,...,179}s over a 182s budget — early checks are cheap, patience grows), and the masked template compare. It even scraped an MFA code back out of the mail HTML and typed it into the page — closing a full verify-by-mail loop.

In this project#

Deferred — needs a mail-capable SUT. The pattern generalizes: any async oracle (a webhook, a queued job) wants snapshot-then-poll-new.
