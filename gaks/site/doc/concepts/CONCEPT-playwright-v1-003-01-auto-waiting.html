Definition#

expect(locator).to_be_visible() does not check once — it retries until the page catches up or the timeout fails the test. Playwright's locators and assertions wait for the condition instead of racing the browser.

Why it exists#

The classic cause of flaky tests is the race between the test and the app: click() before the button renders, assert before the page settles. Explicit sleep() calls are how suites rot. Auto-waiting makes the honest default also the fast one — the test proceeds the moment the SUT is actually ready.

In this repo#

generated/*/pages.py — expect_arrived() is an auto-waiting marker check; every state transition in a journey waits on the SUT's proof, never on a timer.
