Definition#

The closed verb vocabulary inside do: and steps: — act (click, fill, check, select), move (navigate), observe (wait-for, expect), call the API (api, save), pace (dwell). A step is one key with its argument; anything else is refused, named.

Why it exists#

A small closed vocabulary is a grammar a person learns in one sitting — and it is not new. The old framework's TSV interpreter ran the same shape as columns: step, action, field name, label, value, screenshot control, locale overrides, ticket. It was field-proven on juniors; generating code from the same grammar is its compile-time heir — what was a runtime interpreter becomes emitted statements a reader can step through.

Planned siblings from the same harvest: maybe (the ifPresent* idiom — click only if present, never fail), check/wait-any (see CONCEPT-dsl-v1-004-01-the-wait), click-image (see CONCEPT-dsl-v1-008-01-the-image-step).

In this project#

STEP_FORMS in taf/model.py is the whole grammar; SPEC-taf-v1-001-04 TAF-3 is the contract; each form renders in both emitters — parity is asserted by tests/test_parity.py.
