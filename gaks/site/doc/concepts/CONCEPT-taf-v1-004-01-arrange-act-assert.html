Definition#

The shape of a readable test: arrange the preconditions, act once, assert the outcome. Three phases, in that order, visible in the code.

def test_ticket_moves_to_triage(board_page):
    ticket = board_page.new_ticket("the report")   # arrange
    ticket.move_to("triage")                        # act
    expect(board_page.ticket("the report")).to_be_in("triage")  # assert

Why it exists#

A test is documentation that runs. When the three phases are separated, a reader sees what is tested before how; when they interleave, the intent drowns in mechanics. In tafbase the phases come from the model: a journey's setup is its start state, the act is its steps, and the assertions are the state markers and expect-final the emitter writes for every arrival — so generated tests are AAA by construction.

In this repo#

Generated suites under tools/ (lane B-2). Emitted tests carry @concept CONCEPT-taf-v1-004-01-arrange-act-assert.
