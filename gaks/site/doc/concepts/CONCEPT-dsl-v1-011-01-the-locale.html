Definition#

A language dimension on the model's data and locators — one data table per language (table[lang][key]), locator variants per locale, and the occasional behavioral flip (the old framework's Arabic runs reversed slider direction).

Why it exists#

A localized SUT is not one SUT — "Log in" and "Ingresar" are different locators for the same element. The old framework ran 18 languages off xlsx columns and transLabel/transValue row overrides; without the dimension declared, localization testing means a second, drifting copy of the model.

In this project#

Deferred — pairs naturally with CONCEPT-dsl-v1-005-01-the-versioned-locator, whose matrix is (version x language). It lands when a second-language SUT model needs it; until then the model's elements/data are single-locale.

Touch it#

Not implemented — see RESEARCH-taf-v1-002-01, items 5 and 25.
